OV Berlin: expert UX report

https://www.ov-berlin.de/ · 2026-11-09T05:52:17.854Z · mobile, tablet, desktop

Summary

What visitors are trying to do

  • Find films with English audio: reached on mobile in 4 actions, reached on desktop in 1 actions
  • Open a film and find where to get tickets: reached on mobile in 1 actions, reached on desktop in 1 actions
  • Open a cinema's page to see what it shows: reached on mobile in 1 actions, reached on desktop in 2 actions

Where the design makes it harder

Measured

  • Some controls are below 24px and crowded by neighbours.
  • Focused controls are covered by another element: View details for On the Sea, rated 7.1, Add to watchlist, View details for Prime Time, Add to watchli
  • Opening "Open navigation menu (narrow window)" leaves focus outside the panel.

Expert hypotheses

  • Tablet film page pushes screenings below trailer, cast and links
  • "English audio" chip silently sets two filters

What should change first

Measured

  • Give these controls at least 24px, ideally 44px.
  • Keep focused controls clear of sticky bars.
  • Move focus into the panel when it opens.

Expert hypotheses

  • At tablet widths, show the 'Next' card and a showtimes button or the screenings list before the cast and details block. Remove the duplicate Watch Trailer button. Place the poster beside the screenings.
  • Show applied values as removable chips, or in the dropdown labels, e.g. "Language: English" and "Subtitles: none". Add a tooltip or caption on the preset chip describing what it sets.

How we will know it worked

Measured

  • No crowded target is under 24 by 24px
  • No focused control is hidden by a sticky element
  • Focus is inside the panel after it opens

Expert hypotheses

  • At 810x1080 the first screening row or a showtimes button is fully visible in the first view
  • After the chip is selected, each active filter's value is visible as text on the page

Each issue states its own validation method. The agent re-measures on every run.
