Summary

What visitors are trying to do

  • Find films with English audio: reached on mobile in 4 actions, reached on desktop in 1 actions
  • Open a film and find where to get tickets: reached on mobile in 1 actions, reached on desktop in 1 actions
  • Open a cinema's page to see what it shows: reached on mobile in 1 actions, reached on desktop in 2 actions

Where the design makes it harder

Measured

  • Focused controls are covered by another element: View details for On the Sea, Add to watchlist, View details for Prime Time, Add to watchlist, View de
  • "Switch to map view" works with a mouse but does nothing with Enter or Space.
  • Opening "Open navigation menu (narrow window)" leaves focus outside the panel.

Expert hypotheses

  • Empty 'CAST & DETAILS' section on film page
  • Desktop film page leaves large margins and pushes showtimes low

What should change first

Measured

  • Keep focused controls clear of sticky bars.
  • Make this control respond to Enter and Space.
  • Move focus into the panel when it opens.

Expert hypotheses

  • Hide the section when it has no data. Or show a message such as 'No cast details available yet', with a link to the cinema's film page.
  • Use a two-column desktop layout, with film details on the left and the screenings list on the right. Place Tickets next to the time and cinema name.

How we will know it worked

Measured

  • No focused control is hidden by a sticky element
  • Enter or Space produces the same result as a click
  • Focus is inside the panel after it opens

Expert hypotheses

  • No heading is shown without content beneath it
  • At 1440x900 the first Tickets button is visible above the fold
